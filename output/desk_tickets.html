Three new problems the team could not solve, and what you’d need

  1. Money coming in.
    • The problem: the Cash tab shows money only goes out. 103 is stuck $104 short, and there’s no way to take the club’s deposit or record a sale.
    • What you’d need:
      • tools: record_customer_payment, invoice_customer, and a refund tool that requires your approval;
      • a receivables (money owed to the shop) table;
      • a Billing agent.
    • Then the club’s deposit could fund PO #2.
  2. Actually talking to people, and new tickets arriving as part of the chatbot that checked size.
    • The problem: drafts #1 and #2 just sit there, and replies from Tauhid or the club never come back into the system. There’s also no tool to create a ticket.
    • What you’d need:
      • a send-email tool that only works after your approval;
      • an inbox-to-ticket intake (a create_ticket tool);
      • a Front Desk/Intake agent that sorts incoming mail into tickets.
  3. Stock arriving, and time passing.
    • The problem: when Bulldog delivers on 2026-09-05, nothing updates the stock or closes the order. date_today never advances, so nothing ever becomes overdue.
    • What you’d need:
      • a receive_purchase_order tool (adds the stock, marks the order received);
      • a day-advance/scheduler tool;
      • a Receiving agent or a Scheduler.
    • A forecasting angle also works: there’s no sales history table, so no agent can plan fall-semester demand. That would need a sales table and an Analytics agent.
